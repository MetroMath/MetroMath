About us

Welcome to Metro Math

Metro Math was founded after two high school sisters noticed only a few local math competitions for elementary and middle school students. The support of family and friends was what bolstered their success, and they want to share that with the rest of their community! The main purpose of this organization is to allow students to meet people from different schools and get together while having fun and doing math!
