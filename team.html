Founders

Our founders

Thrisha Kalpatthi

Co-Founder

Thrisha Kalpatthi is currently pursuing her Master of Science in Computational Biomedicine and Biotechnology at the University of Pittsburgh. She graduated from Penn State University with a B.S. in Premedicine and will attend Sidney Kimmel Medical College in 2027. Her research interests include computational medicine, surgical outcomes, and using data-driven approaches to improve patient care. A four-time AIME qualifier, Thrisha has a longstanding passion for mathematics and STEM education. As co-founder of Pittsburgh Metro Math, she has helped organize mathematics competitions and educational programs for students across the Pittsburgh area. Outside of academics and research, she enjoys dancing, watching movies, and spending time with friends and family.

Havisha Kalpatthi

Co-Founder

Havisha Kalpatthi is a BS/MD student at the New Jersey Institute of Technology and New Jersey Medical School. She has excelled in math since kindergarten, winning many competitions, qualifying for the AIME, and completing AP Calculus BC in 8th grade. Havisha’s passion for science is evident through her neuroscience research and her leadership of a neuroscience club in Pittsburgh. She also placed 6th at the USA Brain Bee. Passionate about teaching, Havisha dedicates much of her time to helping students from all backgrounds succeed in math. When she’s not in the classroom, you might catch her singing, dancing, or diving into a great book!

Our presidents

Shreyan Bansal

Co-President

Shreyan Bansal is a junior at North Allegheny Senior High School and has loved math ever since he was a kid. He has participated in many math competitions over the years, including the AMC, PAML, MathCounts, and more. Last summer, Shreyan was part of the UPMC Hillman Cancer Center Academy, and researched a way to integrate emotional inteligence into large language models (aka AI chatbots). Some of Shreyan’s hobbies include coding, watching movies, and playing basketball.

Harshini Rajmohan

Co-President

Harshini Rajmohan is a senior at North Allegheny Senior High School. She is passionate about teaching students. Harshini has been mentoring students in math for 2 years, especially at underfunded places such as the Brashear Association. In her free time, she likes to watch movies, read, and listen to music.
